The City of Krishna

Mathura is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India, famous as the birthplace of Lord Krishna.

About Mathura

Mathura lies on the banks of the Yamuna River and has a rich history, temples and traditions.

The city is also famous for its traditional sweet, Mathura Peda.

Famous Places

Krishna Janmabhoomi

A major religious site believed to be the birthplace of Lord Krishna.

Vishram Ghat

A famous ghat on the Yamuna known for its evening aarti.

Dwarkadhish Temple

A beautiful and important temple in the city.

Culture and Festivals

Mathura is famous for Holi, Janmashtami, music, dance, temples and traditional food.
